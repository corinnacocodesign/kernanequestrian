Facilities

Indoor and outdoor arenas available for hire

Our arenas are available for riding clubs, pony clubs, schooling and coaching. Both arenas have excellent Martin Collins surfaces, with international standard show jumps available for competitions and training.

The Centre

Built on generations of showjumping experience

Kernan Equestrian Centre opened in its current form in February 2007 and continues a family tradition that began with the original indoor school built in 1967.

Indoor Arena

Ideal for winter leagues, training shows and coaching sessions.

Outdoor Arena

James Kernan

Profile

James Kernan

James Kernan is a former international and Olympic showjumper from Crossmaglen. He represented Ireland at the Barcelona Olympics in 1992 riding Touchdown and is recognised nationally and internationally as a trainer of show jumpers.
